The Molsom (also spelled Malsom or Morchhom) community is an indigenous ethnolinguistic group and a prominent sub-tribe of the Halam community in Tripura.

Key Community Overview

FeatureDetails
ClassificationSub-group / Dafa of the Halam community
LanguageMolsom language/dialect
ReligionHinduism, Christianity and traditional beliefs

Economy and Livelihood

  • Jhum cultivation
  • Cotton weaving
  • Fishing and basketry

Culture & Festivals

  • Anthar Buthar
  • Risabomb
  • Sengrak worship
